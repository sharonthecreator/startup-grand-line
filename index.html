    The world is split by the Red Line, a continent ringing the globe, and the Grand Line, the sea route around its middle. Founders from the four Blues ride their currents up Reverse Mountain, pick one of seven routes through Paradise, meet at Sabaody, dive beneath the Red Line to the New World — and, with all four Road Poneglyphs read, reach Laughtale. The One Piece waiting there is the Exit.
