    Startup Grand Line

    Crews from four origin seas meet below Founders’ Peak and choose one of seven routes, one for each startup sector. Each route passes island after island, from Pre-Seed to Late Stage, through its own gate in the Market Wall into the Global Waters, and past four beacons to the treasure: Exit Island.

    Click any island and your ship sails there. Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, follow the compass, and open the 📜 chart key to see what each landmark means.

    An illustrative chart inspired by pirate-adventure stories, not investment advice. Company facts are as publicly reported.
